Fix · DocuSign modal

Waiting for signature → signed

A thin wrapper: overlay, header naming the envelope, a status banner, the iframe area where Larry's existing signing link loads, and a footer with an email-it-to-me escape hatch. The banner and the footer button are the two things that flip when DocuSign posts back completion — nothing else in the modal changes, so the transition is quiet.
