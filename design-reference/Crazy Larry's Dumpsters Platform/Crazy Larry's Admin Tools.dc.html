Schedule · week

Drag-to-reassign state showing

Each day column carries a capacity bar above it, because the question is never just “what's on Tuesday” — it's “can Tuesday take one more.” Friday is at 32 of 32, so its column greys out and refuses the drop. The chip being moved goes translucent, a dashed purple target opens in the destination day, and the purple bar across the top is the confirm step — nothing commits on release alone. Seven columns cannot hold a customer name on a phone, so the week restacks there into seven full-width day sections — capacity bar in each day's header, movements as the same rows the day view uses, and the drop target and at-capacity notice inline.

Schedule · day and month

Same segmented control, three densities

Day is the dispatch view — a time gutter, one row per movement, driver on the right, with load-by-driver and the unassigned job beside it. Month trades detail for the capacity read: a bar and a count per day, so Larry can see which weeks he is about to oversell — on a phone it drops the drop/haul chips and keeps just the number and the bar, since 9px labels are below the floor this system sets everywhere else. The phone opens on Day; the owner in the cab wants today, not the month.

Driver management

List · assignment · route view

The assignment panel answers the Round 1 example directly: the unassigned Sanders Basement job, with candidate drivers sorted by the detour it adds rather than alphabetically — Dale is +12 minutes, Marcus is +48 and tips into overtime, Tam has no truck. That is the number that decides it, so it is set large and colored by consequence. The route view stacks an optimized stop list under the map with done stops greyed.

Call log · Quo

Row list with one row expanded

Caller, time, length, then Quo's summary as the widest column — the summary is what makes the log scannable, so it gets the space rather than metadata. Expanding a row opens the timestamped transcript with the audio scrubber beneath it, and a panel of what Quo extracted: which booking it matched, what was asked for, sentiment, and what was promised. Outcome chips reuse the status palette, with orange for anything owed a callback.

Calls I made — flag any of these
Capacity is per day, not per truck
The schedule caps a day at 32 — the whole fleet. If the real limit is trucks and hours rather than cans, the bars should count stops per driver instead.
Detour minutes are invented
Driver assignment ranks by added drive time, which assumes a routing service. Without one it would rank by stop count and rough zone instead.
Quo's extracted fields
Matched booking, request, sentiment and promise are what I assumed Quo returns. Tell me the real fields and the panel changes to match.
