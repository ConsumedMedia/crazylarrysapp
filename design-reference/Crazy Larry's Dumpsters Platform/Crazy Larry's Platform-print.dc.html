What changes on the phone

The rail collapses into a five-item bottom bar with 48px targets. Stat cards drop to two columns, the split panels stack, and the search field keeps full width because searching a can number is the thing Larry does most from the cab.

Nothing is removed — the inventory ledger, movements list, donut, alerts, map and week load are all present in the same order, so a phone glance and a desk glance tell the same story.

Tiles, not a table

The can grid drops from six columns to three, which keeps the ID, size, status and day counter on their own lines at full size. A driver in a cab can read it at arm's length.

Status filters wrap into two rows rather than scrolling sideways — every filter stays visible, because the filter is how you answer “what do I have free right now.”
